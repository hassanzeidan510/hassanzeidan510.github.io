Profile

Engineering from laboratory experiments to molecular-scale insight

I am a Chemical Engineering PhD candidate at Ferdowsi University of Mashhad with an interdisciplinary background in separation processes, bioactive-compound extraction, process modelling, molecular simulation, nanotechnology, and scientific research.

My experience includes five years in a university laboratory, where I contributed to supercritical fluid extraction and hydrodistillation research involving medicinal plants and bioactive compounds. My responsibilities included experimental planning, extraction-process modelling, optimization, interpretation of analytical results, student supervision, and laboratory management.

My current doctoral work focuses on extraction of bioactive components, nano-adsorbent structures, modelling and simulation of nanoparticles, molecular adsorption, and potential nanodrug-delivery applications. I also hold a Doctor of Business Administration from the University of Tehran and assist with scientific article preparation and manuscript development.
